## Investigation boundary

MK is an independent fraud strategy and risk advisory practice. Where formal investigative or forensic work is required, it is delivered through specialist investigative partners. The website should make this capability discoverable without implying that MK itself is a forensic investigation firm.

## Digital / cyber boundary

"Digital, Identity & Cyber-Enabled Fraud Risk" means fraud-specific exposure in digital journeys and technology-enabled deception, including identity misuse, account takeover, phishing, social engineering, impersonation and related fraud controls. It does not position MK as a general cybersecurity, penetration-testing or SOC provider.

## Fraud Readiness position

Fraud Readiness is a flagship route into MK and the intellectual core of the assessment work. It is not the boundary of the firm. A client can engage MK directly for training, internal fraud controls, supplier/third-party fraud, digital/cyber-enabled fraud, AI-enabled fraud, programme design, monitoring or another named service without first completing Fraud Readiness.
