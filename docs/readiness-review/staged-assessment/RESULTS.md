## 1. Why the 534 related practices are never reached

The engine serves the respondent, in order: profile questions; answers to reconfirm after a correction; the queued follow-ups of scenarios whose confirmation reported a gap; tier 1 confirmations; the activities screen; tier 2 and tier 3 confirmations. The accountable-function channel serves confirmations opened by an owner's answer, questions assigned because the respondent did not know, and, after "send to owners", the confirmation questions of unresolved tier 1 and 2 scenarios. No step serves a question whose coverage-map role is "related"; a related practice is asked only when a confirmation waits on it as a prerequisite (3 of 534 in 144 simulated interviews). The coverage map lists them under each scenario, but no routing rule uses that list.

They are therefore unreachable by construction, not by applicability: 379 have no condition of their own and would apply whenever their module applies. By scenario tier: tier 1 247, tier 2 215, tier 3 72; 508 are detail-stage questions.

## 3. Three assessment designs on the same fictional organisations

D0: the current interview, 95 respondent screens. D1: the same interview with no limit. D2: staged in the same session: core (profile, scope, tier 1 confirmations with a ten-scenario consistency sample, one per domain, and the activities screen), breadth (tier 2 and 3 confirmations), then packages for accountable functions: the owners' answers to "I don't know", and for the eight priority gaps the reports present, up to two follow-ups each and, where the gap is partial or not yet working, up to four diagnostic practices; the follow-ups of every governance (D1) and response (D10) gap, for the target operating model; plus up to two practices for each objective no confirmation covers. Gap follow-ups move from the respondent to the functions. D2+ asks the engine's follow-ups for every gap and every diagnostic and coverage practice. Priority: tier, then not in place before set out before partly, then method order.

## 5. Why P6 gave 80.9 at the limit and 63.6 at the end

| P6 (as in the methodology package) | Respondent screens | Follow-ups answered | Tier 1 contradictions established | Tier 1, no treatment | Tier 1, F2 and F4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 95 screens | 95 | 21 | 13 | 88.9 | 80.9 Structured |
| no limit | 275 | 113 | 40 | 88.7 | 63.6 Structured |

The same organisation gives the same answers in both runs. The difference is only how many follow-ups the screen budget allowed: F2 lowers each contradicted confirmation, so the result falls as more contradictions are established. A penalty applied only where a contradiction happens to be found ties the result to the path. A fixed consistency sample (section 4) makes the set of tested confirmations the same in every run for the same organisation, and the same across organisations.
