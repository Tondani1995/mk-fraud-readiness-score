# Unresolved methodological decisions

Reported separately, as MK asked, from the working staged assessment. None of these is decided by build 1.6, and no score is produced until they are. Each entry states the question, what the evidence so far shows, what the harness does meanwhile and what depends on it. Earlier decision sets (MD-1 to MD-16 in `METHODOLOGY-DECISION-PACKAGE.md`, MD-B1 to MD-B11 in `STAGED-ASSESSMENT-AND-SCORING-PLAN.md`) remain open where they are not restated here.

## U-1. The 17 objectives without a scenario confirmation

**Question.** Which of the 17 objectives should the scored frame represent, and how?

**Evidence.** `objectives/OBJECTIVES-REVIEW.md` classifies all 17 against four tests (a control on its own, broad applicability, invisible to every existing confirmation, relied on by a reporting promise). Five pass and are proposed as confirmations on their existing scenarios (P-1); nine stay diagnostic; three are facts or case history. The five changes move the overstated profile by 2.6 points against 24.5 under A+C, because most of the A+C movement comes from 59 maturity and detail practices. P6b and P6c produce identical frame figures; only the consistency check tells an overstated confirmation from weaknesses where no confirmation looks.

**Meanwhile.** Coverage map 1.3 is unchanged. The 17 objectives' practices are asked as coverage questions in function packages and never scored.

**Depends on it.** The candidate frame (K-2), the Essential ten-domain exhibit and the Comprehensive blueprints for risk assessment, procurement, payroll and people risk. Sub-decisions: the tier of FS-D3-10 once it carries training of high-risk roles; whether OBJ-X-11 (independent assurance) is confirmed above a size MK sets; retyping D4-S-07 (P-3).

## U-2. Consistency penalties

**Question.** Should a conflicting consistency answer change a score or a band (F2 ceiling, F4 band limit), and how proportionately?

**Evidence.** In the fictional runs the check tested 10 of 67 eligible confirmations for P6b and found 6 conflicting answers, 3 narrower scope and 1 partial operation; P6c, with identical frames, had 1 conflicting answer; a strong profile had none. A conflicting answer may mean an overstated confirmation or a stricter reading of the follow-up. Narrower scope and partial operation are not contradictions.

**Meanwhile.** Outcomes are recorded and reported with the population, the number tested and the number not tested. F2 and F4 are inactive. Nothing is adjusted.

**Depends on it.** Whether the score can be qualified for contradictions; the disclosure wording; whether the sample size (one per area, ten in all) and order (as confirmations are answered) are adopted, and whether checks may be put to functions rather than the respondent.

## U-3. Snapshot scoring

**Question.** What, if anything, does the Free Snapshot show as a number for a revised assessment, and when?

**Evidence.** The tier 1 indicator (A1) was accepted only as a candidate core indicator, not as an approved overall score. The core stage completes after 52 to 82 respondent screens in the fictional journeys.

**Meanwhile.** The review Snapshot shows no number and says so; the MFRS Free Snapshot is unchanged; the revised family refuses to score.

**Depends on it.** A defined and accepted preliminary methodology for the Snapshot, its label and its relation to the final score.

## U-4. Prioritisation

**Question.** How are issues ranked for Essential's five to eight findings and Comprehensive's five blueprints, and how much may depth burden grow?

**Evidence.** Under a ranking that reads confirmations only, depth answers cannot change the top eight; under an answers-aware ranking they change it for six of seven profiles. The information-gain selection uses no ranking; for the small firm it found fewer gaps than the priority-eight method (5 against 14) because its threshold leaves out tier 2 and 3 diagnostics.

**Meanwhile.** Depth uses information gain with a threshold of 3 and limits of 15 per function, four diagnostics per scenario and two coverage questions per objective. Scenario tiers order the interview only.

**Depends on it.** The ranking rule, whether scenario tiers are a priority classification, the depth threshold and limits, and whether they relax when few confirmations apply.

## U-5. Visibility thresholds

**Question.** When is a result provisional or insufficient, and how do outstanding, declined and "not known by the function" items count?

**Evidence.** The methodology package simulated three rules (`METHODOLOGY-DECISION-PACKAGE.md`). Build 1.6 now distinguishes four kinds of outstanding information: with a function, open in a package, declined with a reason, and not known by the function.

**Meanwhile.** Counts are reported; no threshold is applied.

**Depends on it.** Whether a domain or overall figure may be shown, and the treatment of declined and unknown items in any denominator.

## U-6. Paid-report fulfilment conditions

**Question.** At what stage may each paid report be produced, and what happens to information that arrives later?

**Evidence.** Breadth completes after 96 to 167 respondent screens; function packages add 21 to 130 function screens; the busiest person carries up to 175 screens when the respondent also holds Finance. Declines and "I don't know" answers can leave information outstanding indefinitely.

**Meanwhile.** Essential and Comprehensive continue through the existing engines from the MFRS assessment; nothing is gated on stages.

**Depends on it.** The stage each product requires (for example breadth for Essential, packages for Comprehensive), waiting periods and package expiry, reissue when a correction arrives after issue, how declined and outstanding information is presented, whether the consistency statement appears, and whether depth is free.

## U-7. The scored frame itself

**Question.** Whether frame A (every applicable confirmation, plus any adopted under U-1) becomes the Fraud Readiness Score, with which aggregation, values for non-anchored answers, caps, bands and comparability with MFRS.

**Meanwhile.** MD-1 to MD-16 and MD-B1 to MD-B11 remain open; no calculator for the revised family exists.
