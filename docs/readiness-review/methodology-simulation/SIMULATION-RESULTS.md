# Scoring simulation results (generated)

> HYPOTHETICAL: research simulation, not an approved or production value. Generated by `scripts/research/scoring-simulation/run.mjs` from bank 1.3-review, coverage map 1.3 and review engine build 1.5, on fictional records only. Every treatment, weight, threshold, cap and band here is a hypothesis for MK's decision and is excluded from production. Read with `METHODOLOGY-DECISION-PACKAGE.md`.

## Hypotheses used

| Parameter | Options simulated |
|---|---|
| Frame | A: Option A: every applicable scenario-confirmation control<br>A1: Option A restricted to tier 1 confirmations for the number; tier 2 and 3 confirmations reported beside it |
| Class treatment (non-anchored answers) | exclude: Class-only answers are kept out of the number and reported as a class profile beside it<br>interval: The range the method itself implies: its levelClass puts full at levels 4 to 5 and partial at levels 1 to 3; designed is placed at levels 1 to 2 from the option labels (assumption H-D) (full 80-100, partial 20-60, designed 20-40, absent 0)<br>lower: The lower end of the interval (full 80, partial 20, designed 20, absent 0)<br>midpoint: The centre of the interval (full 90, partial 40, designed 30, absent 0)<br>labelEquivalent: Each class read as the single 0 to 5 label its wording resembles (full as 4 "Consistently operating", partial as 3 "Implemented in key areas", designed as 2 "Partly designed"); shown to demonstrate the effect of a direct mapping, not proposed (full 80, partial 60, designed 40, absent 0) |
| Weighting | equal: Every applicable confirmation weighs the same<br>tier: Tier 1 = 3, tier 2 = 2, tier 3 = 1 (illustrative only) |
| Aggregation | equalDomains: Overall = the mean of the visible domain results, each domain equal<br>pooled: Overall = the mean over every scored confirmation, so domains with more applicable confirmations weigh more<br>none: No overall figure; the domain results and the scenario picture only |
| False comfort | F0: No treatment: a confirmation counts as reported<br>F1: Disclosure only: the confirmation is qualified in the report and the score is unchanged<br>F2: Ceiling: a contradicted confirmation counts no higher than partly in place (level 3, or the partial values)<br>F3: Blend: a contradicted confirmation counts as the mean of its own value and the mean of its answered follow-ups (still one control)<br>F4: Band limit: any contradicted tier 1 confirmation limits the band to Structured; the score is unchanged<br>F2F4: F2 and F4 together, with each contradiction disclosed (F1) |
| Visibility | mfrsComparator: MFRS V1.2 rules on the frame: insufficient if coverage < 70%, unknown >= 30% or exclusions >= 40%; provisional if unknown >= 20%, any exclusion or coverage < 90% (coverage counts unknown answers as answered, as in MFRS)<br>recommended: Outstanding share = (unknown + not reached + applicability not known) / (applicable + applicability not known) over the frame in use. Insufficient at 15% or more; provisional when anything is outstanding. Exclusions by a recorded scope decision never reduce visibility. A domain figure is shown only with 3 or more scored confirmations<br>proposed: Coverage = known / applicable frame; tier 1 coverage tracked separately; exclusions by a recorded scope decision do not reduce visibility; pending applicability does. Insufficient if coverage < 70%, tier 1 coverage < 80%, unknown >= 30% or pending applicability >= 30%; provisional if coverage < 90%, tier 1 coverage < 100%, unknown >= 10% or pending applicability >= 10%. A domain is shown only with coverage >= 70% and at least one scored confirmation |
| Bands (comparator) | Strategic from 80, Structured from 60, Developing from 40, Reactive from 0 |
| Caps (comparator structure) | K1: A tier 1 confirmation at level 0 or 1, or reported not in place: limit Developing<br>K2: A tier 1 confirmation at level 2, or reported partly in place or set out but not working: limit Structured<br>K3: Three or more tier 1 confirmations at level 2 or below, or reported as a gap: limit Developing<br>K4: A visible domain result below 40: limit Developing; below 60: limit Structured |

Defaults unless stated: frame A, interval treatment, equal weighting, equal domains, F0, proposed visibility, caps on. Anchored answers are always level x 20, the existing normalisation.

### 6.2 Where the interview can and cannot reveal a contradiction

The engine asks a scenario's follow-ups only after one of its confirmations is reported as a gap (build 1.5, up to two per scenario). An organisation that reports every confirmation in place is not asked a follow-up, so nothing in its own responses can contradict it. 48 of the 55 tier 1 scenarios have follow-ups.
