# The five candidate confirmations: scheme semantics, routing and distinctness

MK's direction of 11 November (18:33), item 4. The five relationships proposed as P-1 stay candidates; none is applied to coverage map 1.3, no wording, scheme, tier or weight changes, and nothing is scored. Each was tested on three questions: does the candidate's control stop the scheme as the method itself describes it; is the candidate in scope every time the scheme applies; and is it a requirement the scheme's existing confirmations do not already establish.

Routing was tested on the six fictional journeys through the real build 1.6 session (`scripts/research/objectives/candidates.mjs`, output `candidate-confirmations.json`). Wording overlap with the existing confirmations is low for all five (word overlap 0.02 to 0.12), so distinctness below rests on what each question requires, read from its text and level 4, not on wording.

## Consequences if MK adopts the three that hold

D2-I-01 (route-conditional), D4-P-01 and D5-V-01 would each add one first-priority confirmation where they apply, and D5-V-01 changes stage from detail to core. The earlier scoring simulation found that the five together move the overstated profile by 2.6 points; with two of the five not recommended the effect is smaller still. None changes routing elsewhere, and the decision does not block report development: the prototype reports all five today as supporting practices of their schemes.
