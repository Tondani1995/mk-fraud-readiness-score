Where could readiness drift?

Where readiness could drift

These are not weaknesses. Each one is a capability the assessment shows is working, together with the condition it depends on and the change that would put it at risk.

Fraud Detection Capability

Depends on: Fraud analytics owner continuing to own and operate it.

Would signal drift: The cycle slips: Scheduled rule execution at least monthly; monthly tuning review.

Management response: Preserve alert ownership, review discipline and timely escalation as detection coverage evolves.

Third-Party and Supply Chain Fraud Risk

Depends on: Procurement / Vendor Risk continuing to own and operate it.

Would signal drift: The cycle slips: Before engagement; annual refresh for high-risk third parties.

Management response: Preserve supplier due diligence and ownership checks before engagement.

Fraud Culture and Awareness

Depends on: Human Resources / Learning and Development continuing to own and operate it.

Would signal drift: The cycle slips: On every joiner, within the defined completion period.

Management response: Preserve role-specific fraud awareness and timely reinforcement for higher-risk roles.
