## Found while re-verifying the corrections

The first full run on the corrected code (`951ea06`) failed two journeys. Rather than rerun until they passed, both were reproduced and traced, and two further full runs were superseded for the reasons below before the final one.

| Finding | What was established | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| The next prompt could end above the viewport on a phone (J-15 failed in 2 of 6 runs) | After a respondent scrolls down to save the optional detail, the collapsing answers shorten the page and the scroll position is clamped. With the processor busy, Chromium returned the position to the bottom of the regrown page during the entry. A probe that logs every scroll and every script call able to move the page showed no call before the jump, so the browser moved it. | Corrected in `d1a57f3`: the prompt is placed again as the transition ends. Phone placement probe beside a journeys run: 3 and 5 of 180 transitions out of view before the correction, 0 of 180 after. |
| J-10 timed out | A test defect: the changed answer opened the optional detail panel, which the journey did not save. The journeys script also exited 0 when journeys failed. Under load it timed out again, because the panel opened after the journey's fixed allowance. | Corrected in `e6690fc` (which also names the journey and page of every captured error) and `deb0aa1`: every script waits for the panel or the finished save, never a fixed time. |
| The management recording failed | Sending the open questions from the management view removed the button and showed nothing in its place. The old recorder waited for a note that only the Snapshot had and carried on silently after 15 seconds, so the clip delivered with `fb91178` held a 15-second pause at that point. | Corrected in `d3e50c7`: the management view shows which functions hold questions once they are sent.
