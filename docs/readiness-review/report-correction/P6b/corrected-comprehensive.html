Invoices are paid for goods or services not received or not agreed

S03 · procurement · tier 1
How it starts
Overbilling, fictitious deliveries or inflated rates are paid.
Why it matters
Invoices paid without checking approval and receipt are how overbilling and fictitious deliveries get paid.
What the answers show
Three-way match is reported for every invoice, but receipt of goods is reported as recorded before payment for most deliveries only. The match can check an invoice against receipt only where receipt has been recorded, so it cannot operate for every invoice as reported. For services, the person receiving the service is reported as not confirming delivery before payment, which is the receipt the match relies on for service invoices. Service invoices cannot be checked against receipt as reported.
What would interrupt it
Receipt is always recorded before the related invoice is paid. Services confirmed: in place across everything it should cover.
Warning signs
Duplicate or false invoices; Goods or services paid for but not received
