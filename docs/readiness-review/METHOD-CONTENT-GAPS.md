# Method content gaps

MK's direction of 12 November 2026, item 3: where the method is not specific enough to support a recommendation, identify the gap instead of inventing professional judgement. The corrected reports do exactly that: each control's target standard is the method's own wording, and where the method says nothing more the control register marks the gap (codes L, R, C and I in Comprehensive Appendix C; full text in the workbook). Generated by `scripts/revised-reports/content-gaps.mjs` with the rules the reports use.

## What would close them

The largest gap is the in-place scale: most practice questions are answered as in place, partly, set out or not in place, with no description of the target beyond "yes, across everything it should cover" and no records. For the reports to give a specific target design, implementation activities and a success measure for these controls, the method needs, per question, a one-sentence target state, the records that show it operating and, where relevant, its cycle. This is method content for MK to write or approve; the prototype does not invent it. The anchored questions already carry most of it, which is why recommendations for them read more specifically.
