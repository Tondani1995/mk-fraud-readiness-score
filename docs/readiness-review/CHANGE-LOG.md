# Change log of material product decisions

Decisions taken in this candidate, why, and what remains for MK. Owner-reserved decisions (scoring policy, staging isolation) were not taken.

## Method and data

1. **The review track runs beside the scored assessment, chosen per build.** `NEXT_PUBLIC_MK_READINESS_TRACK=review` switches new visitors to the review; the default build is the legacy scored assessment. Nothing is migrated: issued scored records, reports and orders stay in the legacy stack and are never reinterpreted. Reason: the brief keeps legacy production scoring and issued records separate, and the scoring policy is MK's decision.
2. **Engine build 1.5, additive to the rechecked build 1.4.** Four additions, each needed by a multi-person service, with every 1.4 call unchanged: a respondent-only channel for `next()` so owner follow-ups never enter the respondent's interview; the assumptions screen recorded when answered; a `limitation` field on reviewer conclusions; and read-only exports (`labelOf`, `EV_STATES`, `CONCLUSIONS`, `DNAME`) for the adapter. The build is vendored byte-identical and pinned by hash.
3. **Sessions are replayed action logs, not stored state.** The served question is recorded, so replay reproduces exactly what the person saw. A session recorded under a different engine is refused rather than silently reinterpreted.
4. **One report feeds every output.** Interview, Snapshot, owner packages, reviewer workspace, management view, API and export are views of the same engine report, checked for parity by contract and journey tests.
5. **No score anywhere in the review track.** No readiness figure, band, rating, confidence or predicted state. The Snapshot uses four counts, each with a definition and, where meaningful, a denominator.
6. **"I don't know" is a routing decision, not an answer value.** The question goes to the accountable function and stays open; after an unknown inside an area, the respondent may send the rest of that area in one step.
7. **The 95-screen limit is the respondent's only.** Owner answers and corrections do not use it; the profile does. After the limit, owners can still settle questions, including ones their own answers open.
8. **Reviewer records are bound to the answer version.** A changed answer makes earlier evidence and conclusions not current and returns the control to re-review; history keeps the earlier version, value and follow-up. A follow-up can be saved without a conclusion.

## Experience

9. **The review interview reuses the approved instrument,** including the area rail, context panel, response canvas, collapse, confirm and enter transition, and prompt positioning, rather than a new design. The rail shows answers given or where an area was sent, never a tick or share; the context panel shows screens used out of 95 and says it is not a measure of readiness.
10. **Motion never delays saving.** The save starts on the click; the next question enters only after the server has recorded the answer and the exit has finished. Reduced motion skips every phase.
11. **Failure is explicit.** A failed save keeps the selection, says nothing was recorded and offers a retry with the same action ID, which is applied once.
12. **Public copy changes only where the scored description would be untrue** under the review track (hero, journey, product notes, methodology, preview). All replacement copy is in one file for MK's review and is marked not yet approved.
13. **The public preview is generated by the engine** from a fictional organisation and checked by a contract test, so the marketing page cannot show a state the service does not produce.

## Commercial

14. **Prices, entitlements and payment logic untouched.** Under the review track the paid tiers are shown as not available from a review Snapshot instead of offering a purchase the service cannot fulfil. Options for MK are in `COMMERCIAL-RECONCILIATION.md`.

## Environment

15. **Fictional local harness only.** Sessions are files on the server's disk, links stand in for sign-in, and every review route is not found unless the harness is explicitly enabled and the runtime is not production. No Supabase project, credential or customer record is used, because staging isolation is unresolved.

## Corrections after the independent review (contract 1.3.0)

16. **A waiting question states the recorded reason it is waiting.** The reason comes from the answer log and the engine's assignment record, never from a default. Second-stage follow-ups the interview did not reach are no longer described as unknown answers.
17. **A record is bound to its complete ruleset.** Engine, bank, coverage, contract and action schema are stored with the record and checked on every replay; earlier records are read only through an explicit historical reader. A changed bank or coverage map can no longer reinterpret an old record.
18. **The harness storage fails truthfully.** Creating a session is one locked transaction over the link index; a damaged index is reported rather than treated as empty; a failed start leaves nothing behind.
19. **Role credentials never leave through telemetry.** Every telemetry boundary replaces the credential segment of review paths, and review pages run no analytics.
20. **Reviewer drafts are kept until saved or explicitly discarded,** and never attached to an answer that changed after they were written.
21. **The next prompt is placed again as each answer transition ends.** The placement rule is unchanged; it is applied a second time when the transition returns to idle, because the browser can move the page after the first placement (defect below). A failed save changes no question and leaves the page where the respondent is.
22. **Sending the open questions has a visible result on the management view.** Once packages exist, the management view shows which functions hold questions, with the same note as the Snapshot.
23. **A database store for review sessions.** The same record and contract as the file harness, in an isolated Supabase project: append-only answer log, per-session sequence, rulesets stored once, links by hash, report runs with lineage, and access only through functions the server role may run (`DATABASE-DESIGN.md`). The shared preview and production project is refused until B2 is resolved.
24. **A report input contract.** What a reworked report engine consumes, built only by replaying the stored log, identified by its digest, keeping scope, reported practice, uncertainty, evidence and review judgement apart, and carrying no score (`REPORT-INPUT-CONTRACT.md`).
25. **Database tests on an explicitly chosen backend.** `MK_REVIEW_TEST_BACKEND=local` runs the store tests on the embedded database as before; `hosted` runs the same scenarios on the authorised temporary project through the existing RPC interface with the server-side secret key, and refuses any other project, a key of the wrong kind or project, or a key without the expected privileges. Without the secret key, the tests that need it are reported as blocked, never as passed (`DATABASE-DESIGN.md`, Running the tests).
26. **Report input 1.1.0: how each answer version was given.** Every answer version carries `source` (`respondent-answer`, `respondent-correction`, `owner-answer`, `activities-screen`) from the engine's own answer log. Version 1.0.0 documented a `correction` channel that it never produced, so a correction could not be told apart from a first answer (defect below).
27. **Database test cleanup is guaranteed and verified separately.** One finalisation path runs on every exit, purges only sessions the run can show it created, removes temporary users, and confirms each session, link and user is gone; the result is reported apart from the tests (exit code 4 if it cannot be verified). The deterministic fixture is never deleted to make room. Table denial counts only with a recognised permission or not-exposed error. Every output is redacted. The hosted secret key is read only from a private file outside the repository, a long-lived legacy key is refused, and `scripts/readiness-review-hosted-tests.sh` runs the hosted suite.
28. **Answer provenance matched exactly.** Each answer version is matched to the engine's log entry with the same time, value and channel, so a missing entry gives `null` for that version alone (defect below).
29. **Cleanup exceptions.** A record the database test run cannot show it created (no identifying field matches, or only some do) is never purged: the run ends with exit code 5 and the record is confirmed untouched for investigation. SIGHUP is handled; the limits of cleanup after a forced stop, and how to find what remains, are documented.
30. **Report engine design, revision 2.** Four layers (executive decision brief, management analysis, action and accountability register, technical evidence annex); information-gathering sequence kept apart from remediation recommendations and management decisions, with no ranking of fraud risk; evidence levels with no claim of independent validation; attributable reviewer interpretation; revised decisions D1 to D10.
31. **Report engine Phase 1.** The report document contract, input gate, claims ledger, fifteen validation checks with 44 mutation tests, draft copy and a fictional-only draft builder; the end-to-end trace and a draft executive brief for MK review. No report generation, delivery or commercial connection.
32. **Report engine design, revision 3: the product boundary.** MK does not review evidence or verify responses in the standard assessment or the Comprehensive Report; evidence review, verification and specialist conclusions belong to the separately scoped Advisory package. The standard report rests only on the organisation's own responses and the method, and neither its content, its generation nor its release depends on evidence or reviewer records. Release criteria, templates and checks changed to match before Phase 2 continued.
33. **Report input 1.2.0.** Each practice control carries the draft method's own content: the answers it classes as in place, the anchored in-place description, and the fraud risks it names. Additive; the stored log is unchanged and the input without the new field is byte for byte the 1.1.0 input. The S5.1 fixture digest is re-pinned (`506cf9c0…`), with a check that its 1.1.0 projection is still `cb2180f5…`.
34. **Report engine Phase 2: the standard self-reported report.** Rules and templates for all four layers, including scenario analysis: gaps indicated by the responses by area; ten coverage categories with the adaptive routing explained; reported issues and one recommended action per issue that restates the practice as the draft method describes it in place, or is withheld; judgements MK has not recorded withheld explicitly; a concise brief with no repetition and no named control. Twenty checks, 70 mutations, eight golden sessions with pinned digests. Fictional only, draft copy, connected to nothing.
35. **The Advisory record.** The evidence and reviewer functionality, preserved as a separate capability with its own copy and six checks: never cited by the standard report, never a prerequisite for it, and never worded as an MK finding while the reviewer identity is unverified.
36. **Integration assessment: the existing engines and the revised assessment.** Following MK's direction to keep and enhance the existing Snapshot, Essential and Comprehensive engines rather than build a second report architecture: the pipeline as implemented, input compatibility, the scoring assumptions that no longer hold, a proposed versioned methodology for MK approval, minimal changes, a quality plan, versioning, tests and acceptance criteria (`REPORT-ENGINES-AND-REVISED-ASSESSMENT.md`), with a wording-similarity appendix showing that the revised answers cannot be translated mechanically into the 68 MFRS questions. No engine, scoring rule or commercial path changed.
