## Defect exposed by manual owner-quality review

Although the provider response passed the frozen validators, the live Sustainment prose exposed a contract defect. The generic `whyThisMatters` slot still asked:

> Why does the material exposure pattern matter operationally?

That remediation-shaped question caused a strong organisation's assurance priorities to be narrated as a present material exposure, including the sentence:

> The material exposure is concentrated in fraud governance and risk identification, followed by culture and continuous improvement.

The deterministic high-readiness model does not describe those priorities as current control failures. It treats them as operating capabilities to preserve, confirm and resilience-test.

## Repair

The six-slot architecture remains unchanged. For Sustainment only:

- the `whyThisMatters` contract now asks what the strong position depends on and what could cause it to deteriorate;
- the bounded brief now exposes management summaries of assurance coverage, assurance priorities and resilience tests;
- the writer is explicitly prohibited from converting assurance priorities into current material exposure or weakness;
- `SUSTAINMENT_EXPOSURE_AS_WEAKNESS` rejects present-tense material-exposure framing;
- the V1.2 gate proves the exact live-call defect sentence is rejected;
- a legitimate resilience-oriented positive control remains accepted.

Remediation and Mixed interpretation continue to use the original exposure-oriented contract.
