# Questionnaire concept evaluation

Prototype route: `/design-review/concepts` (local/preview only). Same question, substantive labels and operational meanings. Selection is a local demonstration, with no save or branching claim. The final customer experience is one guided instrument combining C's context with B's response motion.

| Criterion | A — maturity ladder | B — dynamic canvas | C — guided instrument |
|---|---|---|---|
| Usability | Order is clear; adjacent stages easy to compare | Large selectable areas, but reading can zigzag | Focused question, orderly responses, context retained |
| Completion speed | Six-step visual invites unnecessary deliberation | Direct selection; good removal choreography | Direct selection plus compact previous-answer confirmation |
| Mobile | Long scale and operational definitions require height | Grid needs a different narrow composition | Compact rows with accessible definition disclosure work naturally |
| Accessibility | Native radios feasible; scale may be mistaken for a score | Native radios feasible; collapse needs focus/locking care | Native radios and domain progress can stay semantically distinct |
| Originality | Familiar capability-scale metaphor | Spatial transformation is distinctive | Organisational coverage rail connects to the final dashboard |
| Professional credibility | Strong if clearly distinguished from readiness | Needs restraint to avoid an app-like novelty effect | Strongest institutional context and orientation |

Decision: C's guided stage, domain coverage rail and stable question anchor, with B's real collapse/removal of unselected options. No separate branching calculator, no live risk conclusion and no scoring numbers on the response options. Meanings remain available before selection via native disclosure controls on mobile and inline on desktop.

This is an expert design evaluation, not a participant usability study. Speed is measured on the final working customer component; the prototype comparison is not a claim that all three have passed production timing or accessibility certification.
