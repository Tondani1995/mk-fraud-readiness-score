# MK Fraud Insights | Confidential Reporting
**Draft pending legal and operational review. Not an active reporting invitation.**

MK invites relevant information about suspected fraud, corruption, scams, cyber-enabled financial crime and material fraud-control failures. MK is an independent fraud risk consultancy, not a law enforcement authority, emergency service or statutory whistleblower protection body.

The channel should permit reports without providing a name or email address. A private receipt will allow secure follow-up. The user must keep this receipt safe. Tor may improve network privacy, but anonymity or legal protections cannot be guaranteed. Never unlawfully access information to make a report.

Submissions must be evaluated only for stated intake purposes. They must not be sold, used for client prospecting, or sent into marketing, HubSpot, website analytics or email attachments. Not every report can be investigated or referred.

Before use, insert an approved POPIA privacy notice covering the responsible operator, lawful processing, cross-border transfers, retention/deletion, rights, legal disclosures and contact route. Document conflicts, escalation, evidence handling, safe attachments and independent security approval. Avoid promising statutory Protected Disclosures Act protection just because MK accepts a report.
