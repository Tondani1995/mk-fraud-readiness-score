# P08 Aurelia — resilience projection (deterministic, zero provider)

Source: `sustainmentPriorities`. Not the `assurance_validation` scenario objects.
Section title: **Control resilience tests**.

## Sustain continuous improvement and fraud risk monitoring readiness

- **Domain:** 
- **Dependency to test:** Fraud risk assessment; Key control inventory
- **What would signal deterioration:** Review overdue, or a key control found lapsed with no remediation owner.
- **Evidence to inspect:** Review scope and schedule,Key control operating evidence sampled in the period,Lapsed or degraded control register,Consolidated report to governance with actions and due dates
- **Effectiveness signal:** Every key control is evidence-tested within the review cycle, with lapses remediated by due date.
- **Review rhythm:** At least annually, and after any material incident or restructuring
- **Traceability:** AP-01

## Sustain fraud risk identification readiness

- **Domain:** 
- **Dependency to test:** Fraud risk assessment method; Documented process inventory
- **What would signal deterioration:** Any material process unmapped, or a mapped residual gap with no owner.
- **Evidence to inspect:** Process inventory identifying material value-bearing processes,Per-process fraud scenario map,Control linkage showing preventive and detective controls,Process-owner sign-off
- **Effectiveness signal:** 100% of material value-bearing processes have a signed fraud map with named controls and residual gaps.
- **Review rhythm:** Refreshed with the fraud risk assessment and on material process change
- **Traceability:** AP-02

## Sustain fraud detection capability readiness

- **Domain:** 
- **Dependency to test:** Process fraud maps; Access to transactional data
- **What would signal deterioration:** A material process unmonitored, or a monitoring cycle missed without escalation.
- **Evidence to inspect:** Red-flag indicator definitions per process,Monitoring output for recent periods,Population reconciliation to source systems,Coverage report against process fraud maps
- **Effectiveness signal:** Monitoring covers all material processes on rhythm with reconciled population totals and routed output.
- **Review rhythm:** Defined monitoring rhythm, at minimum monthly, with continuous capture
- **Traceability:** AP-03
