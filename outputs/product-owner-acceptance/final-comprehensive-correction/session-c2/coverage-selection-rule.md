# Representative capability selection rule

Applied per domain, in strict precedence. Deterministic and reproducible; no AI, no customer
name, no array position, no page-density optimisation.

1. **An assurance priority for the domain** — a deep-dive treatment already exists → posture `DEEP_DIVE_PRIORITY`
2. **A hard-gate control** (methodology significance)
3. **A critical control**
4. **Highest materiality score** among remaining domain controls
5. **Stable control-ID lexical tie-break**

Where a domain matches none of these — normal for a strong profile, where most domains produce
no finding at all — the row carries **position and posture only**, with the note:

> Maintain through normal management review. The assessment records no control requiring
> separate confirmation in this domain.

Nothing is invented to fill the remaining columns. A gate rejects any row that claims proof or
a deterioration signal without a representative capability behind it.

## P08 outcome
3 domains resolve at precedence 1 (the three assurance priorities); 7 fall to the honest
fallback. Postures: 3 deep-dive, 7 maintain.
